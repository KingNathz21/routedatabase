These guides explain the tools and data behind RouteFlow. Live predictions and disruption information come from Transport for London; the route archive is an independent reference and is updated as verified details become available.

How to explore London's bus route archive

Search by route number, place, operator or vehicle details. Switch between current and withdrawn services, then open a route to see its recorded information and TfL stop sequences where available.

A note on accuracy

Clear about what is known.

RouteFlow does not treat missing archive fields as confirmed facts. Some historical operator, garage, vehicle and date details may be unrecorded or awaiting verification. Live arrival and disruption information is supplied by TfL and may change at short notice.
